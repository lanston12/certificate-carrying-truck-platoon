## Claim boundary

The result is a finite-horizon, finite-fleet conditional certificate on uninterrupted normal intervals. It is not a global string-stability, collision-invariance, fallback-stability, field-calibration or tracking-superiority result. The fixed-C1 comparator remains empirically well behaved after C1 applicability ends, but it no longer has analytical authority there. The cell-aware and fixed-C1 fleet RMS values remain 0.6527 m and 0.6531 m.
